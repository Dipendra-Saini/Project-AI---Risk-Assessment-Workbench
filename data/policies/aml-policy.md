## AML-POL-3.1 Customer risk: domestic retail and small business customers
tags: CUS
Domestic retail consumers and small businesses with verified identity are standard-risk customers. Standard customer due diligence applies: identity verification, nature and purpose of the relationship, and ongoing monitoring. Fraud and account takeover are the dominant financial crime risks for retail segments. Risk increases where onboarding is non-face-to-face or the segment is new to the bank.

## AML-POL-3.2 Customer risk: non-resident customers
tags: CUS GEO
Non-resident customers are higher risk because identity, address and source of funds are harder to verify and the customer may be exposed to foreign sanctions or corruption risk. Non-resident onboarding requires documentary verification, screening against sanctions and PEP lists, and an expected-activity profile. Enhanced due diligence applies where the country of residence is a high-risk or monitored jurisdiction.

## AML-POL-3.3 Customer risk: politically exposed persons
tags: CUS
Politically exposed persons (PEPs), their family members and close associates are high risk for bribery, corruption and money laundering. Senior management approval is required to establish or continue a PEP relationship, together with source of wealth and source of funds verification and enhanced ongoing monitoring. Products that attract PEPs, such as private banking and high-value transfers, must be assessed for PEP exposure before launch.

## AML-POL-3.4 Customer risk: money services businesses
tags: CUS TRX
Money services businesses (MSBs), payment institutions and other non-bank financial institutions present elevated money laundering and terrorist financing risk because they move funds on behalf of their own customers, whom the bank does not know. The bank must confirm the MSB's registration and licensing, review its AML programme, and understand its customer base and corridors before offering services.

## AML-POL-3.5 Customer risk: legal entities and beneficial ownership
tags: CUS
For legal-entity customers the bank identifies and verifies beneficial owners holding 25 percent or more of ownership and one individual with significant control, consistent with the FinCEN Customer Due Diligence Rule. Complex or opaque ownership structures, nominee shareholders and shell companies are high-risk indicators. Products for corporate customers must specify how beneficial ownership will be captured.

## AML-POL-3.6 Customer risk: non-profit organisations
tags: CUS
Non-profit organisations can be misused for terrorist financing, particularly where they operate in or send funds to conflict zones or high-risk jurisdictions. A risk-based approach is required: understand the organisation's purpose, funding sources, beneficiaries and geographies, and monitor for transfers inconsistent with its stated activities.

## AML-POL-4.1 Geographic risk: new markets and foreign exposure
tags: GEO
Any product, customer segment or vendor that introduces exposure to a jurisdiction outside the domestic market requires a geographic risk assessment. Factors include the jurisdiction's AML/CFT framework, corruption levels, sanctions exposure and the bank's ability to perform due diligence locally. Domestic-only activity carries low geographic risk.

## AML-POL-4.2 Geographic risk: high-risk and monitored jurisdictions
tags: GEO
Jurisdictions subject to a call for action or under increased monitoring by the FATF are high risk. Activity involving a call-for-action jurisdiction requires enhanced due diligence and FCRM senior approval and is presumed outside risk appetite unless strong mitigation is demonstrated. Jurisdictions under increased monitoring require enhanced due diligence and enhanced transaction monitoring.

## AML-POL-5.1 Delivery channel risk: non-face-to-face onboarding
tags: CHN
Online and mobile onboarding without physical presence increases impersonation, synthetic identity and mule-account risk. Non-face-to-face channels require electronic identity verification with document and liveness checks, device and IP intelligence, and limits on activity until verification is complete. Branch onboarding is the lowest-risk channel.

## AML-POL-5.2 Delivery channel risk: agents and intermediaries
tags: CHN TPV
Where customers are introduced or served through agents, brokers or other intermediaries, the bank relies on a third party's conduct and controls. Agents must be onboarded with due diligence, trained, monitored and subject to audit. Agent networks in cash-intensive or cross-border businesses are high risk.

## AML-POL-5.3 Delivery channel risk: APIs and embedded finance
tags: CHN TPV
Distribution through APIs, banking-as-a-service and embedded finance partners means the end customer may be onboarded by a partner. The bank remains responsible for its AML obligations. Partner programmes require contractual control requirements, data access for monitoring, and the ability to suspend the partner.

## AML-POL-6.1 Transaction risk: volume and value
tags: TRX
Transaction risk rises with expected monthly value and volume. FCRM uses value bands: below 1 million USD per month (standard), 1 to 10 million (elevated), 10 to 50 million (high) and above 50 million (very high). Monitoring scenarios and thresholds must be calibrated to the product before launch.

## AML-POL-6.2 Transaction risk: instant and irrevocable payments
tags: TRX PRD
Instant, real-time and irrevocable payments reduce the time available to detect and stop fraud and money laundering. Products offering instant settlement require real-time screening, fraud scoring, and velocity limits. Authorised push payment fraud and mule activity are the primary typologies.

## AML-POL-7.1 Controls: effectiveness and residual risk
tags: PRD CUS GEO CHN TRX TPV
Residual risk is inherent risk reduced by the effectiveness of controls. Control effectiveness is assessed for design and for operation, based on testing results. Controls reduce but never eliminate risk; no assessment may record zero residual risk. Where controls are new or untested, operating effectiveness is assumed to be low until tested.
