## FW-FATF-R15 FATF Recommendation 15: new technologies
tags: PRD CHN
Countries and financial institutions should identify and assess the money laundering and terrorist financing risks that may arise from the development of new products and new business practices, including new delivery mechanisms, and the use of new or developing technologies. The risk assessment should take place prior to launch, and appropriate measures should be taken to manage and mitigate the risks.

## FW-FATF-RBA FATF risk-based approach for banks
tags: CUS GEO PRD CHN TRX
Under the FATF risk-based approach, banks identify country or geographic risk, customer risk, and product, service, transaction or delivery channel risk, and apply mitigation proportionate to the risks identified. A risk-based approach does not mean zero failures; it means resources are directed where risk is highest.

## FW-FFIEC-RA FFIEC BSA/AML risk assessment
tags: PRD CUS GEO TRX
The FFIEC BSA/AML Examination Manual expects a bank's risk assessment to identify specific risk categories, including products, services, customers, entities, transactions and geographic locations, and to analyse the information to assess risk within these categories. The assessment should be updated when new products, services or customer types are introduced.

## FW-WOLF-RA Wolfsberg risk assessment methodology
tags: PRD CUS GEO CHN TRX TPV
Industry methodology described by the Wolfsberg Group assesses inherent risk, evaluates the design and operating effectiveness of controls, and derives residual risk. The methodology should be documented, consistent across the organisation, and repeatable.

## FW-OFAC-FCC OFAC Framework for Compliance Commitments
tags: GEO CUS TPV
The OFAC framework identifies five essential components of a sanctions compliance programme: management commitment, risk assessment, internal controls, testing and auditing, and training. The risk assessment should cover customers, products, services, supply chain, intermediaries, counterparties, transactions and geographic locations.

## FW-FINCEN-CDD FinCEN Customer Due Diligence Rule
tags: CUS
Covered financial institutions must identify and verify the identity of beneficial owners of legal entity customers, understand the nature and purpose of customer relationships to develop a customer risk profile, and conduct ongoing monitoring.

## FW-BCBS-MLFT Basel Committee sound management of ML/FT risks
tags: CUS GEO
The Basel Committee expects banks to identify, assess and understand money laundering and financing of terrorism risks, with appropriate governance by the board and senior management, customer acceptance policies and risk-based monitoring.

## FW-TPRM Interagency third-party risk management guidance
tags: TPV
Banking organisations should manage risks from third-party relationships across the life cycle: planning, due diligence, contract negotiation, ongoing monitoring and termination, with rigour proportionate to the level of risk and the criticality of the activity.

## FW-SR117 Model risk management
tags: PRD
Supervisory guidance on model risk management expects models to be documented, validated with effective challenge, and monitored on an ongoing basis. Scoring engines and AI components used in risk decisions fall within scope.
