## SAN-POL-2.1 Prohibited and comprehensively sanctioned jurisdictions
tags: GEO TRX
The bank does not provide products or services involving comprehensively sanctioned jurisdictions, directly or indirectly, unless a specific licence applies. Any change that could route funds, customers or vendors to or from such a jurisdiction must be escalated to the Sanctions Officer before committee review. Geo-blocking and screening must prevent access from these jurisdictions.

## SAN-POL-2.2 Screening of customers, counterparties and payments
tags: CUS TRX GEO
All customers, beneficial owners and payment counterparties are screened against sanctions lists at onboarding, on list updates and in real time for payments. New products must demonstrate that every party and message field relevant to sanctions is available for screening. Cross-border payments must carry complete originator and beneficiary information.

## SAN-POL-2.3 Sanctions risk assessment
tags: GEO CUS PRD TPV
Consistent with the OFAC Framework for Compliance Commitments, the sanctions risk assessment considers customers, products, services, supply chain, intermediaries, counterparties, transactions and geographic locations. A new product, geography or vendor triggers a refresh of the sanctions risk assessment for the affected line of business.

## SAN-POL-3.1 Virtual assets and sanctions evasion
tags: PRD GEO
Virtual asset services carry heightened sanctions evasion risk because wallets can be controlled from sanctioned jurisdictions and funds can be layered quickly. Wallet screening, blockchain analytics and IP geolocation controls are mandatory for any virtual asset exposure.
