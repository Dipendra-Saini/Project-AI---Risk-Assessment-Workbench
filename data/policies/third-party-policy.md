## TPR-POL-2.1 Vendors and partners in financial crime processes
tags: TPV
A third party that touches customers, payments or financial crime controls requires due diligence proportionate to its risk and criticality, consistent with interagency guidance on third-party risk management. Where no third party is involved, third-party risk is minimal. Payment processors require review of their sanctions and AML controls.

## TPR-POL-2.2 Outsourced KYC and transaction monitoring
tags: TPV CUS TRX
The bank may outsource the performance but not the accountability for customer due diligence or transaction monitoring. Outsourced KYC or monitoring requires documented standards, quality assurance sampling, management information, right-to-audit clauses and an exit plan. Vendors located offshore require data access and sanctions review.

## TPR-POL-2.3 Custody and safeguarding by third parties
tags: TPV PRD
Where a third party holds customer assets, including virtual assets, the bank must assess the custodian's financial crime controls, segregation of assets and insolvency protections. Custody arrangements are high impact because failure affects customer assets directly.

## TPR-POL-3.1 Bribery and corruption risk in third parties
tags: TPV CUS
Intermediaries, agents and vendors that interact with government officials or operate in high-corruption jurisdictions require anti-bribery due diligence, contractual anti-corruption clauses and review of commissions and fees.
