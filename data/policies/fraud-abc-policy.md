## FRD-POL-2.1 Fraud risk in digital channels
tags: CHN CUS
Digital onboarding and payments are exposed to account takeover, synthetic identity, mule accounts and authorised push payment scams. Expected mitigants are device fingerprinting, behavioural analytics, fraud scoring, confirmation-of-payee and step-up authentication.

## FRD-POL-2.2 First-party and promotional abuse
tags: PRD CUS
Sign-up incentives, credit features and cashback can attract first-party fraud and abuse. Eligibility checks, velocity limits and monitoring for linked accounts are required.

## FRD-POL-3.1 Anti-bribery and corruption
tags: CUS TPV GEO
Products and relationships involving public officials, state-owned entities, or high-corruption jurisdictions carry bribery and corruption risk. Gifts, hospitality, commissions and intermediary payments must be reviewed, and PEP screening applied.
