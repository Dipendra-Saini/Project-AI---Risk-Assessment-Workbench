## NPA-POL-2.1 When a financial crime risk assessment is required
tags: PRD CUS GEO CHN TRX TPV
A financial crime risk assessment is required before launch for every new product, material feature change, process change affecting customer due diligence or monitoring, new vendor performing a financial crime relevant function, entry into a new geography, and offering to a new customer segment. This reflects FATF Recommendation 15 and supervisory expectations for new, modified or expanded products. No change may go live before a risk committee decision.

## NPA-POL-2.2 Decision outcomes
tags: PRD
The risk committee may approve, approve with conditions, reject or defer a change. Conditions must be specific, owned and time-bound, for example a transaction limit for the first 90 days or a post-launch control test. Deferred changes return to FCRM with the committee's questions.

## NPA-POL-3.1 Product risk: cross-border value transfer
tags: PRD GEO TRX
Products that move value across borders are exposed to money laundering, sanctions and terrorist financing risk, and depend on the quality of correspondent and partner controls. Cross-border features require payment transparency (travel rule data), sanctions screening of all parties and corridor-level risk review.

## NPA-POL-3.2 Product risk: cash, prepaid and anonymity-enhancing features
tags: PRD
Cash-intensive products, prepaid or stored-value instruments and features that reduce transparency of the customer or the source of funds are high risk for money laundering. Loading limits, identity verification before use, and monitoring of reloads and cash-outs are expected mitigants.

## NPA-POL-3.3 Product risk: virtual assets
tags: PRD TRX
Products that allow customers to buy, sell, hold or transfer virtual assets carry very high money laundering, fraud and sanctions risk. Blockchain analytics, wallet screening, travel rule compliance and clear custody arrangements are required. Virtual asset products are presumed High inherent risk at minimum.

## NPA-POL-4.1 Post-launch monitoring
tags: PRD TRX
Approved changes are subject to post-launch monitoring of the risk indicators named in the assessment, typically for 90 days. Material deviations from the expected-activity profile trigger a re-assessment.
